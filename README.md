# ICU Early-Warning AI

## Research Project

An AI-assisted clinical early-warning system designed to analyze
longitudinal ICU patient data and estimate the risk of clinical
deterioration before a critical event occurs.

## Objective

The goal is not simply to display patient vitals.

The system will study patterns across multiple physiological
measurements over time and attempt to identify patients who may be
at increased risk of deterioration.

## Core Signals

The system may analyze signals such as:

- Heart Rate
- Blood Pressure
- SpO2
- Respiratory Rate
- Temperature
- Laboratory measurements
- Patient demographics
- Clinical history
- Temporal trends

## Planned Research Components

1. Data preprocessing
2. Missing-data handling
3. Feature engineering
4. Temporal pattern analysis
5. Machine learning models
6. Risk prediction
7. Model evaluation
8. Explainable AI
9. Patient-level risk visualization
10. Experimental comparison of models
